# Required Intake Fields Per Document Type

Reference checklist for `/estate-documents`. Check the attached intake answers against this list before drafting each document. A document type drafts only once its required fields are present — missing fields get flagged by name to the attorney, never guessed or filled with a plausible-sounding placeholder value.

Optional fields improve the draft but never block it. Required fields block that document type only — a missing field in one document never blocks the other three.

---

## Last Will and Testament

**Required:**
- Testator full legal name
- Testator county and state of residence
- Marital status (and spouse's full legal name, if married)
- Children (full legal names and dates of birth, if any — including from prior relationships)
- Executor (primary): full legal name, relationship
- Executor (alternate): full legal name, relationship
- Residuary beneficiaries: who receives the estate after specific bequests, and in what shares

**Required only if the testator has minor children:**
- Guardian for minor children (primary): full legal name, relationship
- Guardian for minor children (alternate): full legal name, relationship

**Optional:**
- Specific bequests (named items or amounts to named people/organizations)
- Personal property distribution wishes
- Digital asset instructions
- Funeral/disposition wishes

---

## Healthcare Power of Attorney (Advance Directive for Health Care)

**Required:**
- Principal full legal name (must match the name used on the will and the other documents)
- Healthcare agent (primary): full legal name, relationship
- Healthcare agent (alternate): full legal name, relationship
- Treatment preferences on file: at minimum, life support / resuscitation preference

**Optional:**
- Agent and alternate contact information
- Additional treatment preferences (pain management, artificial nutrition/hydration, organ donation)
- Conditions under which the agent's authority begins (immediately on signing vs. only on a determination of incapacity — flag as attorney-to-confirm if not stated, since this is a state-law-dependent default)

---

## Financial (Durable) Power of Attorney

**Required:**
- Principal full legal name (must match the name used on the will and the other documents)
- Agent (primary): full legal name, relationship
- Agent (alternate): full legal name, relationship
- Scope of powers granted (e.g., banking, real estate, tax matters, business operations, gifting authority) — at minimum, one category must be specified

**Optional:**
- Agent and alternate contact information
- Effective date: immediately vs. springing (on a determination of incapacity) — flag as attorney-to-confirm if not stated, since the default varies by state
- Compensation terms for the agent
- Specific limitations or exclusions on the agent's authority

---

## HIPAA Authorization

**Required:**
- Patient (principal) full legal name and date of birth (must match the name used on the other documents)
- Authorized recipients of health information: full legal name(s) and relationship — commonly the healthcare agent and alternate, but confirm rather than assume they're identical

**Optional:**
- Scope limitation (all records vs. a specific category)
- Expiration date or event (defaults to "until revoked" if not specified — flag as attorney-to-confirm)

---

## Cross-document consistency — check every time, not just once

Before presenting any drafted set, confirm across every document drafted in the same session:

- The principal/testator's legal name is spelled identically in every document.
- Agent and alternate names, and their relationship labels, are spelled identically wherever they recur (e.g., the healthcare agent named in the Healthcare POA matches the name and spelling used in the HIPAA Authorization, if the same person).
- Agent/alternate ordering (who is primary, who is alternate) is consistent across documents where the same people appear.
- Dates are formatted the same way across every document in the set.

If the intake answers contain a spelling or naming inconsistency for the same person across documents (e.g., "Kim Alvarez" in one answer and "Kimberly Alvarez" in another), do not silently pick one — flag it and ask which is correct before drafting.
