# /engagement-letter — Engagement Letter Drafter

> ⚠️ AI-ASSISTED DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED
> This output was generated by an AI assistant. It has not been reviewed by a licensed attorney. Do not send or have a client sign this letter without your independent review and approval. Engagement letters are binding agreements. This is not legal advice.

Draft a retainer and engagement letter from intake data in the matter folder. If the attorney has saved their own engagement-letter template, it fills that template's own wording rather than a generic letter. The attorney reviews, customizes, and sends it — the client never sees an AI-generated version without attorney sign-off.

**This skill reads files only. It does not send anything.**

## Invocation

```
/engagement-letter [matter folder path or client name]
```

---

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Locate intake data

Use the Filesystem connector to find the matter folder. Look for:
- `intake.md` or `intake-summary.md` — client name, contact info, matter description
- Prior email with client (search Gmail: `from:[client email] OR to:[client email]` last 30 days) to capture agreed fee terms

If no intake file exists, ask the attorney to provide:
1. Client full name and address
2. Matter description (what you are being retained to do)
3. Fee type: flat fee / hourly / contingency
4. Fee amount or rate, and payment terms
5. Any scope exclusions (what you are NOT handling)

### Step 2 — Locate candidate templates

Before drafting, check whether the attorney has their own engagement-letter template on file — this skill fills in the attorney's own wording rather than a generic letter whenever one is available. Use the Filesystem connector to check, in order:

1. **The matter folder itself** (a matter-specific override, if the attorney saved one there).
2. **A `templates/` folder at the top level of the connected Filesystem workspace** (e.g. `~/Matters/templates/`) — the standard place to keep a template that gets reused across every matter.

In each location, match filenames case-insensitively for any of: `engagement`, `retainer`, `fee agreement`, `fee-agmt`, `feeagmt`, `representation agreement`, with extension `.docx`, `.doc`, `.md`, `.txt`, or `.rtf`. Note every match as a candidate — don't read the contents yet. If nothing matches, proceed with an empty candidate list; Step 5 falls back to the built-in template.

### Step 3 — Determine fee structure

Ask the attorney to confirm the fee arrangement if not clear from intake files:

| Fee type | Key terms needed |
|---|---|
| Hourly | Hourly rate, billing increment, retainer deposit amount |
| Flat fee | Total fee, payment schedule, what triggers additional fees |
| Contingency | Percentage (pre-suit vs. post-suit), cost deduction order, client costs |

### Step 4 — Select and read the template

Skip this step if Step 2 found no candidates.

- **One candidate:** read it.
- **Multiple candidates:** disambiguate using the fee type confirmed in Step 3 (e.g. prefer a filename containing "contingency" when the matter is contingency-fee), or ask the attorney which one to use.

Read the selected file's contents, then run two checks before drafting:

**Extraction check** — if the Filesystem connector returns no usable text (common with some `.docx`/`.doc` files), do not guess at the template's content and do not silently fall back to the built-in template. Tell the attorney:
> "I found `[filename]` but couldn't extract readable text from it. Could you paste the template text directly, or save a `.md` or `.txt` copy in the same location?"

**Completeness check** — even when extraction produces text, echo back the section headings you found and ask the attorney to confirm nothing was dropped or reordered before you draft from it:
> "I'm using `[filename]` as your template. It has these sections: [list headings in order]. Does that match your template, or is anything missing?"

Only proceed to Step 5A once the attorney confirms, or once they've supplied usable pasted text.

### Step 5 — Draft the engagement letter

**Step 5A — Using the attorney's own template (if one was confirmed in Step 4):**

Use the attorney's own section structure, headings, and clause language as the base document. Fill in the variable fields — client name, matter description, fee terms, scope language — from the intake data and Step 3 fee confirmation, following the same drafting rules below. Do not add any clause or section that isn't already present in the attorney's template; if the template appears to be missing something the built-in template below would normally cover (e.g. a termination clause), flag it to the attorney in Step 6 rather than inserting it yourself.

**Step 5B — Built-in default template (no usable attorney template found):**

```
[Date]

[Client full name]
[Client address]
[Client email]

Re: Engagement Letter — [Matter description]

Dear [Client first name],

Thank you for choosing [Firm name] to represent you. This letter confirms the terms of our engagement.

SCOPE OF REPRESENTATION

We are retained to represent you in connection with [matter description — be specific]. Our representation is limited to this matter. We are not retained to advise you on [any related matters outside scope, e.g., "tax implications" or "any appeals"].

FEES AND PAYMENT

[HOURLY:]
Our fee for this matter is $[rate] per hour, billed in [increment, e.g., one-tenth of an hour] increments. You are asked to provide a retainer deposit of $[amount] upon signing this letter. We will bill against the retainer and replenish it when it falls below $[threshold]. Unused retainer funds will be returned to you at the conclusion of the matter.

[FLAT FEE:]
Our fee for this matter is $[amount], payable as follows: $[deposit] upon signing and $[balance] upon [milestone or date]. This fee covers [scope]. Additional fees may apply if [specify out-of-scope triggers].

[CONTINGENCY:]
We will represent you on a contingency fee basis. Our fee is [X]% of any recovery if the matter resolves before filing suit, and [Y]% of any recovery if suit is filed. All costs advanced by this office will be deducted from the recovery [before / after] our fee is calculated. If there is no recovery, you owe no attorney's fee, but you remain responsible for costs advanced on your behalf.

COSTS AND EXPENSES

In addition to our fees, you are responsible for all out-of-pocket costs and expenses incurred in connection with this matter, including filing fees, service of process, deposition costs, expert fees, and similar expenses. We will [advance costs on your behalf / require your pre-approval for costs exceeding $X].

YOUR OBLIGATIONS

To allow us to represent you effectively, you agree to: (1) keep us informed of any developments relevant to your matter; (2) respond to our communications promptly; (3) provide complete and accurate information; and (4) make payments as agreed.

COMMUNICATION

Our primary contact for this matter will be [attorney name]. You can reach us at [phone] or [email]. We aim to respond to calls and emails within [1–2 business days].

TERMINATION

You may terminate this engagement at any time by written notice. We may withdraw from representation as permitted by applicable rules of professional conduct. Upon termination, you will be responsible for fees and costs incurred through the date of termination.

NO GUARANTEE OF OUTCOME

We will represent you diligently and professionally. However, we cannot guarantee any particular outcome. Results depend on facts, law, and circumstances outside our control.

GOVERNING LAW

This agreement is governed by the laws of [State]. Any disputes arising from this engagement shall be resolved in accordance with [State] rules of professional conduct.

If these terms are acceptable, please sign and return a copy of this letter along with your [retainer deposit / signed authorization]. We look forward to working with you.

Sincerely,

[Attorney name]
[Bar number]
[Firm name]
[Address]
[Phone]
[Email]

---

ACKNOWLEDGED AND AGREED:

Client Signature: _________________________ Date: ___________

Print Name: _________________________
```

After drafting from the built-in template, tell the attorney they can skip this fallback next time:
> "This draft uses the kit's built-in template since I didn't find one of yours. If you'd like your own wording used automatically on future matters, save it as `templates/engagement-letter-template.md` (or `.txt`/`.docx`) at the top of your Matters folder."

**Drafting rules (apply to both 5A and 5B):**
- Fill brackets from the matter folder or intake data; leave any unfilled bracket visibly marked `[CONFIRM WITH ATTORNEY]`.
- Use the correct fee section and delete the others.
- Keep scope narrow and specific — vague scope is a malpractice risk.
- Do not include contingency fee percentages without confirming the attorney's standard rates.
- State bar rules on contingency fees vary — note this to the attorney if applicable.
- Never introduce a clause, section, or term not already present in the source (attorney's template or built-in template) — flag anything missing in Step 6 instead of adding it yourself.

### Step 6 — Present draft and flag gaps

Show the full draft with a checklist of any missing or unconfirmed items:

> "Here is the engagement letter draft. The following items need your confirmation before sending: [list]. Would you like any changes?"

### Step 7 — Save to matter folder (optional, with confirmation)

> "Would you like me to save this as `engagement-letter-draft-[date].md` in [matter folder path]?"

Do not write any file without explicit confirmation.

## What This Skill Does Not Do

- It does not determine what fee arrangement is appropriate for your jurisdiction or practice area.
- It does not verify compliance with your state bar's specific retainer requirements — review applicable rules of professional conduct before sending.
- It does not send or store any signed version.
- It does not add clauses to your own template that weren't already there — missing pieces are flagged, not invented.
